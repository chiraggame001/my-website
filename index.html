How is CGPA calculated?

CGPA is calculated using the credit-weighted average of your grade points.

CGPA = Σ (Credit × Grade Point) ÷ Σ Credits

For example, a subject with 4 credits and a grade point of 8 contributes 32 points to the calculation.

CGPA to Percentage

This calculator shows an estimated percentage using CGPA × 9.5. Your university may use a different conversion formula, so always check your official academic rules.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is CGPA?

CGPA stands for Cumulative Grade Point Average. It represents your average academic performance.

How do I calculate CGPA?

Multiply each subject's grade point by its credits, add the results, and divide by the total credits.

Can I calculate multiple semesters?

Yes. Click "Add Semester" and enter the subjects, credits and grade points for each semester.

Is CGPA × 9.5 always correct?

No. Different universities and institutions can use different conversion formulas.
